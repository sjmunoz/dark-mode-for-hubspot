# Privacy Policy — Dark Mode for HubSpot

Dark Mode for HubSpot collects no data.

- It does not read, store or send the content of HubSpot pages.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or remote code.
- It stores one setting (dark mode on or off) in your browser with `chrome.storage.local`. This setting does not leave your browser.

The extension changes only the colors of pages on `app.hubspot.com` and the HubSpot regional app domains.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/sjmunoz/dark-mode-for-hubspot/issues
